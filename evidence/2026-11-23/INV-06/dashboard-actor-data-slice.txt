- Scope interpretation: these are club-wide staff views under the accepted single-club model, not member-self routes. The manifest has no row-owner scope for these aggregate/list APIs; that must not be confused with member ownership proof. The interaction map lists endpoint-specific roles, not a Dashboard page-level role allowlist. `Dashboard.tsx` calls `fetchKpis` unconditionally with no role gate in the component. If Dashboard is rendered for coach/reception, the KPI route manifest and current RBAC policy do not permit those roles and the request is expected to fail closed; actual role-to-page reachability and displayed error behavior were not exercised end-to-end.
